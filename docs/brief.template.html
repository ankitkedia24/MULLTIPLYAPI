01 What we have already built

A dedicated integration service that reads our live ERP and pushes our item master to your endpoint PUT /v2/items/sync-data, authenticated with x-api-key, exactly as documented.

  • Live and hosted by us. Deployed on our own infrastructure and monitored around the clock — no manual upload, no spreadsheet, no daily intervention from either side.
  • Always current. The complete catalogue re-syncs every night and stock levels refresh every 30 minutes, so a WhatsApp buyer always sees real, present-day availability and pricing.
  • Built for scale. Items are sent in batches with automatic retry and exponential backoff, and we honour Retry-After — we will never overwhelm your API.
  • We read your responses properly. Your partial-failure envelope is parsed and every entry in errors[] is mapped back to the exact ISBN, so any data issue is corrected at source within minutes rather than investigated for days.
  • Continuously delivered. Every code change is automatically tested and released to production, so the integration stays healthy without scheduled downtime.

03 Testing already completed

  • 32 automated tests covering field mapping, validation, retry behaviour and error handling — all passing on every release.
  • Full-scale rehearsal. Our entire catalogue was pushed to a local replica of your endpoint, built strictly to your published specification: 38,513 items across 129 batches, every batch accepted, zero errors, in 74 seconds.
  • Repeat-run verified. Re-syncing the same catalogue updates the existing records rather than duplicating them, confirming correct create-or-update behaviour on syncId.
  • Pre-flight validation mirrors your own checklist — required fields, numeric prices and globally unique sync IDs — so invalid rows never reach your API. In the rehearsal it correctly held back two titles with incomplete pricing in our records.

04 What we need from Mulltiply

Our API key

A production x-api-key — and a sandbox key first, if you provide one. This is the only outstanding item between us and go-live.

And five short confirmations:

  1. Rate limits and maximum batch size. We currently send 300 items per request, spaced 500 ms apart. Tell us your preferred limits and we will match them.
  2. GST of 0. Books are GST-exempt in India. Please confirm 0 is accepted, or tell us which GST group code you expect instead.
  3. Variant labels. We send Binding and Language in place of Size and Colour_Name. Please confirm these display correctly to buyers.
  4. Delisting. The API has no delete operation. How should a title be retired — is re-syncing it with availableQuantity: 0 the accepted convention?
  5. Row indexing. In partial-failure responses, is errors[].row 0-based or 1-based? We handle both, but would prefer to be certain.
